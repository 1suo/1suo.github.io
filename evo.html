Evolution-based SVG generator: you are the selection pressure. The genome is a typed combinator graph — numbers, colors, shapes, groups. A handful of combinators (fields, transforms, repetition, styling, layering) compose into the whole image; everything from "how many petals" to "how stroke width varies from top to bottom" is an expression of x, y, or the repetition index, not a hardcoded knob.

Click the parent cell to reroll its children. Click a child to make it the new parent. "Random Parent" starts a new lineage, "Undo" steps back one selection.
