Evolution-based SVG generator: you are the selection pressure. The genome is a typed combinator graph — numbers, colors, drawables. The only primitive is a literal path (points + per-segment curvature genes); circles, stars and arcs are just point configurations the sampler writes into it, so every outline is vertex-evolvable. Numbers build fields (x, y, i, noise, sin…), colors blend, and combinators compose: repetition with five placement modes, field warps, planting along outlines, clipping, kaleidoscopes, merged multi-subpath fills with even-odd holes, gradients, blend modes.

Expression runs deep: structural params can themselves be genes driven by fields (paramIns); variant re-runs a subtree with seeded divergence (gene duplication); recur/self lets a body invoke itself; some lineages carry dev rewrite rules — a growth program that expands the graph before drawing (an L-system layer). Repeats and plantings carry a drift gene: every copy keeps the shared structure and promoted expressions, but its literal genes draw a seeded per-copy variation — clones (drift 0) and divergent swarms (drift 1) are both one mutation apart, and any single gene freezes by being promoted to an expression.

Click the parent cell to reroll its children. Click a child to make it the new parent. "Random Parent" starts a new lineage, "Undo" steps back one selection.
